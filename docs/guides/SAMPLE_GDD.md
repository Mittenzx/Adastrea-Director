# Sample Game Design Document - "Quantum Shift"

**Game Title:** Quantum Shift  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** 2025-11-08  
**Author(s):** Adastrea Director Team

## 1. Executive Summary

### High Concept
A fast-paced 3D action platformer where players manipulate quantum states to phase through obstacles and defeat enemies across parallel dimensions.

### Unique Selling Points
- Innovative quantum phase mechanics that let players exist in multiple states simultaneously
- Mind-bending level design that changes based on quantum state
- Tight, responsive controls with a high skill ceiling
- Beautiful cel-shaded art style with neon accents

### Target Audience
Core gamers aged 16-35 who enjoy challenging platformers with unique mechanics (fans of Celeste, Portal, Super Meat Boy)

### Platform(s)
- PC (Steam)
- PlayStation 5
- Xbox Series X/S
- Nintendo Switch (potential future port)

---

## 2. Game Overview

### Genre
3D Action Platformer with Puzzle elements

### Visual Style
Cel-shaded graphics with vibrant colors. Each quantum state has a distinct color palette:
- **Normal State:** Warm oranges and reds
- **Phase State:** Cool blues and purples
- **Quantum State:** Electric greens and magentas

Heavy use of particle effects to show quantum transitions.

### Audio Style
Electronic/synthwave soundtrack with dynamic layers that change based on quantum state. Sound effects have distinct characteristics in each state, creating an audio puzzle element.

### Core Experience
Players should feel a sense of flow and mastery as they chain quantum shifts to navigate increasingly complex levels. The game rewards precision, timing, and creative problem-solving.

## 3. Gameplay

### Core Gameplay Loop

1. Enter a level section
2. Analyze obstacles and enemy patterns
3. Plan quantum state transitions
4. Execute precise jumps and shifts
5. Reach checkpoint or level end
6. Repeat with increased difficulty

### Game Mechanics

#### Primary Mechanics

**Quantum Phase Shift**
- Press Phase button to enter Phase State
- In Phase State, player becomes semi-transparent and can pass through certain objects
- Phase State has a 3-second duration with a 5-second cooldown
- Entering Phase State creates a "quantum echo" at your starting position

**Double Jump**
- Standard double jump available in all states
- Jump height: 3 meters (first jump), 2 meters (second jump)

**Quantum Dash**
- Quick burst of movement in any direction
- 8-meter range
- 2-second cooldown
- Can dash mid-air

#### Secondary Mechanics

**Quantum Echoes**
- Each Phase Shift leaves an echo that can trigger pressure plates
- Echoes last 10 seconds
- Maximum 3 echoes can exist simultaneously
- Strategic echo placement solves puzzles

**Time Dilation Fields**
- Special areas where time moves differently
- Slow-motion fields (50% speed): Better precision for difficult sections
- Fast-motion fields (200% speed): Increased challenge and speedrun potential

### Player Abilities

**Base Abilities (Available from start)**
- Run: 8 m/s base speed
- Jump: 3m height
- Phase Shift: Pass through quantum-permeable objects
- Quantum Dash: Quick directional movement

**Unlockable Abilities (Progression)**
- Triple Jump: Unlocked after Level 5
- Extended Phase: 5-second phase duration (Level 10)
- Quantum Echo Control: Remotely activate echoes (Level 15)
- Superposition: Exist in both states simultaneously for 1 second (Level 20)

### Progression Systems

**Level Progression**
- 25 main levels across 5 worlds (5 levels per world)
- Each world introduces new mechanics and hazards
- Completing levels unlocks new abilities

**Star System**
- 3 stars per level based on:
  - Completion (1 star)
  - Time (1 star - beat par time)
  - Collection (1 star - collect all quantum cores)
- Stars unlock bonus challenge levels

**Cosmetic Progression**
- Unlock character skins by completing challenges
- Unlock particle effects with star collection
- Unlock music tracks by finding secret areas

## 4. Story and Narrative

### Setting
A research facility studying quantum mechanics accidentally tears a hole between dimensions. The facility exists simultaneously across multiple quantum states, creating impossible architecture and dangerous anomalies.

**Time Period:** Near-future (2045)  
**Location:** The Helix - a massive underground quantum research complex

### Story Synopsis
Dr. Aria Chen, a quantum physicist, is trapped in the facility when a catastrophic experiment goes wrong. She must use the facility's quantum phase technology to navigate the collapsing dimensional barriers, rescue her colleagues, and prevent the quantum anomaly from expanding beyond the facility.

### Main Characters

#### Protagonist: Dr. Aria Chen
- **Background:** Brilliant quantum physicist, age 28, known for her unconventional approaches
- **Motivation:** Save her mentor and prevent quantum collapse from destroying the city above
- **Personality:** Determined, quick-witted, refuses to give up

#### Antagonist: The Quantum Singularity (not a person)
- **Background:** The result of the failed experiment - a growing anomaly consuming reality
- **Motivation:** N/A (force of nature)
- **Threat:** Will expand and consume the entire facility and city if not stopped

#### Supporting Characters
- **Dr. Marcus Webb:** Aria's mentor, trapped in a quantum loop
- **QUBIT:** AI assistant that helps guide Aria through the facility
- **Elena Santos:** Security chief trying to evacuate survivors

### Story Beats

1. **Opening:** Aria enters the lab just as the experiment goes critical
2. **Inciting Incident:** Quantum tear opens, Aria gains phase abilities
3. **Rising Action:** Navigate facility sections, rescue colleagues, learn about the anomaly
4. **Climax:** Reach the quantum core and reverse the experiment
5. **Resolution:** Facility stabilizes, but quantum abilities remain with Aria

## 5. World Design

### World Overview
The Helix facility is a 50-story underground complex. The quantum anomaly has created overlapping dimensional layers, making the facility's architecture shift between states.

### Key Locations

#### World 1: Research Labs
- **Description:** Clean, modern laboratory spaces with glass walls
- **Purpose:** Tutorial area introducing basic mechanics
- **Key Features:** Transparent walls become solid in Phase State, laser grids, pressure plates

#### World 2: Quantum Core Chamber
- **Description:** Massive cylindrical chamber with the quantum reactor
- **Purpose:** Introduce advanced phase mechanics
- **Key Features:** Energy beams that damage in Normal State but are safe in Phase, moving platforms

#### World 3: Maintenance Tunnels
- **Description:** Industrial corridors with pipes and machinery
- **Purpose:** Focus on precision platforming
- **Key Features:** Spinning hazards, steam vents that propel player, narrow passages

#### World 4: Data Center
- **Description:** Server rooms with holographic displays
- **Purpose:** Puzzle-heavy world with echo mechanics
- **Key Features:** Data streams that create paths, firewalls requiring multiple echoes

#### World 5: Containment Sector
- **Description:** The epicenter of the quantum anomaly
- **Purpose:** Final challenge combining all mechanics
- **Key Features:** Reality distortion, shifting gravity, dimensional tears

### World Rules
- Quantum-permeable materials (marked with blue glow) can be phased through
- Phase State protects from energy-based hazards but not physical ones
- Time flows normally for player but can be affected by specific fields
- Quantum echoes persist across state changes

## 6. Level Design

### Level Flow
Linear progression through worlds with optional branching paths for secrets. Each world culminates in a challenge level that tests mastery of that world's mechanics.

### Level Structure

#### Level 1-1: "Awakening"
- **Objective:** Learn basic movement and first Phase Shift
- **Challenges:** Simple phase walls, basic jumps
- **Rewards:** Phase ability tutorial, first quantum core
- **Par Time:** 1:30
- **Length:** 2 minutes average

#### Level 1-2: "Echoes of Reality"
- **Objective:** Master quantum echo creation
- **Challenges:** Echo-activated doors, multiple phase sequences
- **Rewards:** Echo mechanic unlocked
- **Par Time:** 2:00
- **Length:** 3 minutes average

#### Level 2-1: "Core Reaction"
- **Objective:** Navigate around active quantum reactor
- **Challenges:** Energy beams, phase timing, precision jumps
- **Rewards:** Quantum Dash ability
- **Par Time:** 3:00
- **Length:** 4 minutes average

#### Level 5-5: "Singularity" (Final Level)
- **Objective:** Close the quantum tear
- **Challenges:** All mechanics combined, boss-like encounter
- **Rewards:** Story completion, Superposition ability
- **Par Time:** 10:00
- **Length:** 15 minutes average

### Environmental Storytelling
- Computer terminals show logs of the accident
- Quantum distortions increase as you approach the core
- Visual damage to facility shows timeline of collapse
- NPC dialogue (via QUBIT) provides context

## 7. Game Systems

### Economy System
Not applicable - no traditional economy in this game.

### Collectibles System

**Quantum Cores**
- 3 per level, 75 total
- Required for 100% completion
- Often placed in hard-to-reach areas
- Emit distinctive sound and glow

**Data Logs**
- 25 scattered throughout the facility
- Provide lore and backstory
- Not required for completion

### Checkpoint System
- Automatic checkpoints every 30-45 seconds of gameplay
- Manual checkpoints at puzzle solutions
- Death respawns at last checkpoint with no penalty
- For speedruns, checkpoints can be disabled

## 8. User Interface

### HUD Elements
- **Phase Energy Bar:** Top left, shows phase cooldown
- **Ability Icons:** Top center, shows available abilities and cooldowns
- **Quantum Core Counter:** Top right, shows collected cores in current level
- **Timer:** Bottom right (optional, for speedrunning)

### Menu Structure
```
Main Menu
├── Start Game
├── Continue
├── Level Select (unlocked after beating World 1)
├── Options
│   ├── Graphics
│   ├── Audio
│   ├── Controls
│   ├── Gameplay (assist mode, timer display)
│   └── Accessibility
├── Extras
│   ├── Character Skins
│   ├── Statistics
│   ├── Data Logs
│   └── Credits
└── Quit
```

### Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad | Description |
|--------|----------|---------|-------------|
| Move | WASD | Left Stick | 8-directional movement |
| Jump | Space | A | Hold for variable jump height |
| Phase Shift | Shift | X | Toggle phase state |
| Quantum Dash | Right Click | B | Dash in movement direction |
| Ability Menu | Q | Y | Quick ability swap |
| Pause | Escape | Start | Pause game |

## 9. Audio Design

### Music
**Main Theme:** "Quantum Collapse" - Epic electronic orchestral piece

**World Themes:**
- World 1: "Research" - Calm, mysterious synth
- World 2: "Core" - Pulsing, energetic beats
- World 3: "Maintenance" - Industrial, mechanical sounds
- World 4: "Data" - Glitchy, digital sounds

### Sound Effects

**UI Sounds:**
- Menu navigation: Soft beeps
- Selection: Quantum shimmer
- Error: Distorted buzz

**Gameplay Sounds:**
- Phase shift in: High-pitched "whoosh"
- Phase shift out: Low-pitched "thump"
- Quantum dash: Sharp "zip"
- Echo creation: Ethereal chime
- Checkpoint: Reassuring tone

**Environmental Sounds:**
- Ambient facility hum
- Quantum distortion crackling
- Energy beam hums
- Machinery clanks

### Voice Acting
**QUBIT (AI Assistant):** Male voice, helpful and occasionally sarcastic. Provides hints and story context.

**Environmental Audio Logs:** Various scientists' voices telling the story of the accident.

## 16. Risk Assessment

### Technical Risks
- **Risk:** Phase mechanic may cause performance issues with many objects
  - **Mitigation:** Early optimization, limit phase-able objects per scene
- **Risk:** Input latency affects tight platforming
  - **Mitigation:** Extensive controller testing, multiple input buffers

### Design Risks
- **Risk:** Difficulty curve too steep, alienates casual players
  - **Mitigation:** Implement assist mode, extensive playtesting with varied skill levels
- **Risk:** Phase mechanic becomes repetitive
  - **Mitigation:** Introduce variations and combine with other mechanics regularly

### Business Risks
- **Risk:** Crowded platformer market, hard to stand out
  - **Mitigation:** Focus on unique quantum mechanics, target speedrun community
- **Risk:** Development overruns budget/timeline
  - **Mitigation:** Aggressive scoping, cut non-essential features, maintain feature freeze after production
